## ATS rules of thumb (from Simplify/JobRight research)
1. Mirror the exact job-title phrase in your resume summary (e.g. "Software Engineer Intern").
2. Use the same skill spelling the posting uses (TypeScript not TS, CI/CD not CICD).
3. Put 3-5 posting keywords in your top 3 bullets, not buried in skills.
4. Quantify: users, releases, hours saved, data volume.
5. One page. Standard headings: Experience, Education, Skills, Projects.
6. No tables/columns in the PDF - single column, plain text, standard fonts.
